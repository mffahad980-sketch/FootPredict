Prediction principles

Quality over quantity.

01

Evidence-led

No pick should be published without enough reliable supporting data.

02

Honest confidence

Confidence scores are estimates, not probabilities unless calibrated against historical results.

03

Public record

Keep losing picks visible. Measure long-term performance, not only winning streaks.
